📖 How To Play

🎯 Objective

Guess the mystery football player by asking yes/no questions about their characteristics!

🎮 Gameplay

  • You'll see a board of 24 famous footballers
  • A mystery player is secretly chosen
  • Ask questions about position, nationality, league, and more
  • Click on players to flip them face-down when eliminated
  • The fewer questions you ask, the better!

💡 Tips

  • Start with broad questions (e.g., "Is the player a forward?")
  • Narrow down by league or nationality
  • Use physical traits when few players remain
  • Click players to eliminate them from your board

🏆 Scoring

Try to guess in as few questions as possible! Can you do it in under 5?
